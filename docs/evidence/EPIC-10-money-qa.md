# EPIC-10 — Employee + Finance money paths

## Employee

1. Sign in (demo) as **Employee**.
2. `/dashboard/pay` — YTD, statement list, open `/dashboard/pay/pay-2026-10-15` for earnings/taxes/deductions.
3. Add/remove a bank account; download W-2 HTML.
4. Confirm total rewards (base + bonus + employer benefits).
5. `/dashboard/expenses` — submit a report with receipt filename.
6. `/dashboard/procurement` — catalog request.
7. Agent: **Explain my last paycheck** → statement detail. **Submit $42 software expense** → lands in Expenses as Submitted.

## Manager

1. Persona → **Manager**.
2. `/dashboard/expenses` — approve pending.
3. `/dashboard/procurement` — approve request.

## Finance

1. Persona → **Finance Analyst**.
2. `/dashboard/expenses` — approved queue (reimbursement).
3. `/dashboard/procurement` — issue PO number after approve.
4. `/dashboard/ledger` — chart of accounts + balanced draft journal.
5. `/dashboard/planning` — Beacon Planning baseline/stretch vs actual.
6. `/dashboard/close` — toggle month-end tasks; reload persists.
